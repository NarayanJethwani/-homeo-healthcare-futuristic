# KEP-1 Flagship Source Dossiers

**Status:** Planning review required

**As-of date:** 2026-07-26

**Publication and RAG posture:** Unchanged and inactive

## Purpose

These dossiers define the source and reviewer prerequisites for the eight KEP-1
flagship entities. They do not contain approved claims, rewritten clinical
content, publication approval, or production retrieval authorization.

## Source policy

Authoritative clinical webpages are registered for citation and evidence
assessment only. Their text must not be copied, bulk-extracted, embedded, or
republished until a rights reviewer records permission for that use.

The Kent 1905 and Boericke 1901 editions reuse the existing governed
public-domain evidence in the Materia Medica registry. They may enter a governed
extraction workflow, but extracted passages remain draft evidence until
independent editorial and clinical review.

## Evidence boundaries

- Disease and symptom pages must cover definitions, differential diagnosis,
  red flags, diagnostic limits, conventional care, and evidence limitations.
- Lab pages must explain that an isolated result does not establish a diagnosis
  and must preserve laboratory-specific reference-range context.
- Remedy pages must distinguish historical Materia Medica descriptions from
  evidence of efficacy.
- Remedy pages must state product-safety and delayed-care risks using current
  regulatory sources.
- No dossier may include guaranteed diagnosis, guaranteed cure, replacement of
  urgent or conventional care, patient-specific treatment recommendations, or
  potency and dosing instructions.

## Editorial assignments

Each entity requires four explicit contributor assignments:

1. clinical author;
2. independent clinical reviewer;
3. evidence reviewer;
4. rights reviewer.

All 32 roles remain unassigned. The assignment gate rejects missing identities
and rejects any clinical author who is also the independent clinical reviewer.

## Next gate

The program may move from planning to draft authoring only after:

- all contributor IDs are recorded;
- author and independent-review identities are distinct;
- source rights remain valid for the intended use;
- coverage gaps are resolved;
- the human program owner records approval of the assignments.

This gate does not authorize publication or production RAG.
