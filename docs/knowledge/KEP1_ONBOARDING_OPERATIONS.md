# KEP-1 Onboarding Operations

**Program:** Knowledge Expansion Program, flagship cohort

**Status:** Roster incomplete; controlled drafting remains blocked

**Publication and production RAG authority:** None

## Purpose

This operating layer converts the KEP-1 contributor requirements into an
auditable staffing plan without putting personal or credential evidence into a
source-controlled report. It measures whether the private governance registry
has enough qualified people to cover the pilot; it does not invite, verify,
approve, or assign anyone.

## Minimum operating roster

KEP-1 requires eleven distinct operating seats:

- four clinical authors: gastroenterology, dermatology, laboratory medicine,
  and homeopathy subject matter;
- four separate independent clinical reviewers covering the same domains;
- one evidence-methodology reviewer;
- one source-rights reviewer;
- one verified program owner.

One person occupies no more than one operating seat in this staffing-readiness
report. This creates genuine review capacity and prevents a broadly credentialed
person from making an understaffed roster appear complete. The contributor
intake gate continues to enforce author/reviewer separation on every dossier.

## Private intake procedure

For each proposed person, an authorized human operator must record the following
in the private governance registry:

1. immutable identity and evidence of verification;
2. current role-appropriate credential and private evidence location;
3. specialty and editorial-role eligibility;
4. conflict-of-interest declaration;
5. editorial-independence attestation;
6. AI-assistance disclosure agreement;
7. source-use policy agreement.

The program owner must be independently identity-verified. After the roster is
covered, the owner proposes and approves all 32 entity-role decisions through
the contributor-intake process. Self-approval remains forbidden.

## Privacy boundary

The committed operations artifact contains only aggregate counts, fixed seat
identifiers, coverage state, and fixed action codes. It must never contain:

- names;
- staff IDs, ORCIDs, GitHub identities, or other identity values;
- credential identifiers or evidence locations;
- conflict details;
- contact information.

Those records belong in the access-controlled governance registry. They must not
be supplied in pull-request descriptions, issue comments, build logs, or public
reports.

## Advancement rule

Roster coverage is necessary but not sufficient. Controlled drafting remains
blocked until the contributor-intake manifest separately passes all 32
assignment decisions. Neither roster coverage nor intake approval grants
publication, graph approval, patient-specific advice, or production RAG
authority.
