# V2.14.0-B Evidence Scoring Methodology

This document outlines the deterministic evidence metadata scoring and priority retrieval blending methodology.

---

## 1. Canonical Evidence Metadata Model

All governed knowledge objects in the Homeo Healthcare CMS contain a structured `KnowledgeEvidenceProfile`:

### 1.1 Evidence Strength
Quantifies the level of scientific/empirical backing for the clinical statements in the article:
- **`very-high`** (Weight Score: `100`): Level-A (Meta-analyses, systematic reviews, robust multi-center clinical trials).
- **`high`** (Weight Score: `80`): Level-B (Controlled clinical trials, robust cohort studies).
- **`moderate`** (Weight Score: `60`): Level-C (Observational studies, case-control studies).
- **`low`** (Weight Score: `40`): Level-D (Case series, expert opinion papers).
- **`very-low`** (Weight Score: `20`): Level-E (Anecdotal records, historical notes).

### 1.2 Source Quality
Categorizes the reliability and authority of the publication source:
- **`authoritative`** (Weight Score: `100`): Pharmacopoeias, international consensus guidelines, official pharmacopoeial monographs.
- **`peer-reviewed`** (Weight Score: `85`): Articles published in peer-reviewed clinical journals.
- **`primary`** (Weight Score: `65`): Original historical texts (e.g., Hahnemann's writings).
- **`secondary`** (Weight Score: `40`): Textbook compilations and standard manuals.
- **`unverified`** (Weight Score: `10`): Self-published blogs, unreferenced websites, or gray literature.

### 1.3 Review Expiry Policy
Determines retrieval behaviors once review intervals expire:
- **`flag-only`**: Neutral contribution. Flag warning is displayed to administrators, but no search score penalty or exclusion is applied.
- **`ranking-penalty`**: Applies a ranking demotion to search priority.
- **`exclude-from-ai`**: Excludes the document from AI search contexts once overdue/expired.
- **`exclude-from-all-search`**: Excludes the document from all searches (except admin view) once overdue/expired.

---

## 2. Priority Blending Formula

The priority blending logic normalizes both base relevance and priority retrieval scores to a `[0.0, 1.0]` scale, computing the blended final score using a weighted sum:

$$\text{Final Score} = \text{Normalized Relevance} \times 0.85 + \text{Normalized Priority} \times 0.15$$

Where:
- **`Normalized Relevance`**: Relevance score derived from keyword and semantic vector search, clamped within `[0.0, 1.0]`.
- **`Normalized Priority`**: Calculated deterministic retrieval priority score (0–100) divided by 100, clamped within `[0.0, 1.0]`.

---

## 3. Retrieval Expiry Policy Context Matrix

At the configured exclusion threshold (default: `"expired"`), documents are retrieved or filtered out according to their policy and context:

| Expiry Policy | AI Context (`ai-clinical-context`) | Manual Clinical (`manual-clinical-search`) | Public Search (`public-search`) | Admin Search (`admin-search`) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **`flag-only`** | Eligible | Eligible | Eligible | Eligible |
| **`ranking-penalty`** | Eligible (with penalty) | Eligible (with penalty) | Eligible (with penalty) | Eligible (no penalty) |
| **`exclude-from-ai`** | **Excluded** | Eligible | Eligible | Eligible |
| **`exclude-from-all-search`** | **Excluded** | **Excluded** | **Excluded** | Eligible |

---

## 4. Citation Completeness Check
We enforce structural checks on citations:
- Minimum length constraint: Citations must contain at least 10 characters.
- Duplicate detection: Duplicate references are identified and subtracted from the completeness count.
- Blank/Empty string filtering.
