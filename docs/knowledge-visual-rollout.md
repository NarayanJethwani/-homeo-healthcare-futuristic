# Homeo Healthcare Knowledge Visual Rollout

## Delivery standard

Every topic receives three reviewed images:

1. **Understand** — a clear, clinically appropriate concept or anatomy visual.
2. **Recognise** — a symptom, pattern, test, or lived-context visual without alarmist imagery.
3. **Support** — a lifestyle, monitoring, or next-step visual without making a treatment claim.

Artwork contains no generated text or logos. The website overlays the approved Homeo Healthcare logo and label, while a server-rendered social card supplies the exact logo, topic title, and campaign copy for link previews.

## Quality gate before each batch

- Clinical reviewer confirms the imagery does not imply diagnosis, urgency level, cure, or treatment efficacy.
- Visual reviewer confirms anatomy, cultural inclusivity, accessibility alt text, and Homeo Healthcare brand treatment.
- SEO check confirms the share-card title, canonical URL, Open Graph image, and Twitter large-image card.
- Mobile check confirms all three visual cards remain legible and correctly cropped.
