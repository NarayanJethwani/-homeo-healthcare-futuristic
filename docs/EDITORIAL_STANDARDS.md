# Editorial Standards & Clinical Guidelines

This document defines the editorial rules and clinical content verification standards for the Unified Clinical OS platform.

## 1. Required Sections for Knowledge Articles
Every clinical knowledge article (remedies, diseases, symptoms, or lab tests) must include:
- **Title and Overview**: Structured summary of the entity.
- **Clinical Importance / Why It Matters**: Pathophysiological context.
- **Homeopathic Perspective**: Individualized constitutional considerations and miasmatic background.
- **Evidence Level**: Explicit grading based on clinical trials, classical materia medica, or clinical experience.
- **Citations & References**: Verifiable links to primary literature or classical texts.

## 2. Citation Protocols & Verifiability
- **No Fake References**: Do not hallucinate or use dummy references. Every citation must map to a real, published paper (e.g. PubMed ID), classical Materia Medica text, or Dr. Jethwani's verified clinical archives.
- **Explicit Provenance**: Use unique, traceable citation IDs (e.g., `CIT-0001`) that link directly to the bibliography ledger.

## 3. Medical Safety & Tone Guidelines
- **Safety Disclaimer**: The permanent sticky notice must remain visible: *“Clinical review required — do not auto-prescribe.”*
- **No Definitive Diagnostics**: Talk in terms of "functional organ loads", "reserve capacity", and "constitutional predispositions" rather than absolute statements.
- **No Cure Claims**: Avoid promising "cures" or "guarantees". The tone must be objectively scientific, emphasizing the self-regulatory nature of homeopathic treatment.

## 4. Editorial & Reviewer Workflow
1. **Draft / AI-Assisted**: Content created or imported into the staging database.
2. **Medical Review**: Peer review by qualified clinical practitioners.
3. **Clinical Validation**: Verification against clinical cases or repertorization benchmarks.
4. **Published**: Approved for live query retrieval.
5. **Archived / Deprecated**: Outdated records retired from query results.

## 5. Update & Audit Cadence
- **Bi-Weekly Sync**: Content databases must be audited every two weeks for spelling, formatting, and broken links.
- **Annual Scientific Audit**: A comprehensive review of all clinical guidelines must occur annually to integrate new emerging research.
