# Clarke 1904 Clinical Repertory Remedy Mapping Safety Report

This document reports on the mapping safety audit of John Henry Clarke’s remedy abbreviations to the canonical clinical remedy dictionary.

## Mapping Stats & Resolution Rates

The safety audit calculated the following separate resolution rates for remedy tokens:

* **Total Remedy Token Occurrences**: 12,041
* **Resolved Occurrences**: 9,653
* **Unresolved Occurrences**: 2,388
* **Unique Tokens in Map/Text**: 3,607
* **Unique Tokens Resolved**: 1,453
* **Unique Abbreviation Resolution Rate**: **40.28%** (1,453 / 3,607)
* **Occurrence-Weighted Resolution Rate**: **80.17%** (9,653 / 12,041)
* **Target Dictionary Mapping Pairs**: 1,266
* **Unique Dictionary Targets**: 350
* **Dictionary Target Resolution Rate**: **99.05%** (1,254 / 1,266)

*Note on divergence*: The unique token resolution rate is lower (40.28%) because the raw OCR text contains thousands of unique typos and low-frequency scanner errors that only occur once or twice. However, because standard high-frequency remedy abbreviations map successfully, the clinical occurrence-weighted resolution rate remains high (80.17%).

### Safety Model Implementation

1. **Scoring Exclusion**: Any rubric matching these 12 unresolved target abbreviations has those specific remedy entries omitted from the `relatedRemedies` scoring array. They can never contribute to clinical repertorization scores.
2. **Search-Only Preserves**: The unresolved tokens are preserved in the database inside the `remedyEntries` raw array with `gradeInfo.status = "unresolved"` and `normalizedGrade = undefined`.
3. **Disclosure Notices**: The user interface exposes a safety disclosure indicating that the remedy abbreviation is unmapped and requires manual clinical verification.
4. **No Silent Approvals**: Unresolved keys are blocked from defaulting to approved or influencing rankings, coverage, or constitutional/miasmatic loads.
