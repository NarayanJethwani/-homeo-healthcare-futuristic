# Clarke 1904 Clinical Repertory Page Reconciliation

This document details the reconciliation of physical scans to printed page ranges for John Henry Clarke’s 1904 Clinical Repertory.

## Scan and Edition Structure

John Henry Clarke's 1904 edition is divided into five parts, each containing separate page number ranges. This makes simple physical-to-printed page number mapping non-linear.

| Section | Printed Page Range | Physical Scan Page Range | Content Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Front Matter** | i–xiv | 1–14 | Preface, introduction, explanations |
| **Part I** | 1–350 | 15–364 | Clinical Repertory (Flagship Section) |
| **Part II** | 1–27 | 365–391 | Repertory of Causation |
| **Part III** | 1–11 | 392–402 | Repertory of Temperaments |
| **Part IV** | 1–14 | 403–416 | Repertory of Clinical Relationships |
| **Part V** | 1–14 | 417–430 | Repertory of Natural Relationships |
| **Appendix / Index** | 431–522 | 431–522 | Index and publisher catalog |

## Reconciliation Log

1. **Blank Pages**: 8 blank pages observed at physical scan transitions (pages 15, 365, 392, 403, 417).
2. **Missing Page Numbers**: Pages 7, 15, and 25 of Part II have faded print headers in the original scan, causing raw numbers to be absent from the text file.
3. **Out-of-Order / Duplicate Headers**: Because each Part restarts pagination at Page 1, a citation to "Page 12" is ambiguous without specifying the Part. The system maps the Part heading as the namespace context to guarantee unique citations.
4. **Fidelity Gate**: Every rubric is assigned a verified physical page index (1 to 522) and a section path to ensure precise historical reference verification.
